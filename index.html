About Our Community Garden

Green Haven Community Garden is a shared neighborhood space where residents come together to grow fresh vegetables, herbs, and flowers. We practice sustainable gardening and promote environmental education.

How to Join and Grow

Becoming a member is open to all local residents, whether you are an experienced gardener or planting your very first seed. We provide shared tools, water access, and friendly guidance.

  • Submit a membership application online.
  • Attend a brief orientation session on garden rules.
  • Choose your individual or shared garden plot.

For more details on plots and fees, read our Community Garden Handbook and Rules

Upcoming Community Events

We host seasonal workshops, community workdays, and harvest celebrations throughout the year. Neighbors of all ages are welcome to join in and learn new skills.
